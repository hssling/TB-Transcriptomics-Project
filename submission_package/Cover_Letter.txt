**RE: Submission of Manuscript "Dynamic Transcriptomic Signatures in Tuberculosis: Validating Rapid Normalization Kinetics"**

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit our original research article for consideration in *Clinical Infectious Diseases*.

Tuberculosis (TB) treatment monitoring currently faces a critical "blind spot" due to the slowness of sputum culture conversion. In this study, we analyzed longitudinal transcriptomic data from 367 patients to validate a molecular alternative.

**Key Findings:**
1.  **Rapid Triage**: We demonstrate that host RNA biomarkers significantly normalize (p < 0.0001) as early as **Week 1** of treatment, providing a real-time metric of drug efficacy weeks before culture results.
2.  **Biological Validation**: We characterize the recovery of a "Suppressed" T-cell homeostatic module, proving that cure is not just the absence of inflammation but the restoration of immune health.

Given CID's focus on clinically relevant insights, we believe this work offers a timely contribution to the field of non-sputum diagnostics and personalized medicine.

This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. The authors declare no conflicts of interest.
