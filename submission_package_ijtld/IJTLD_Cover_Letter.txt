Subject: Submission of Original Article "Dynamic Transcriptomic Signatures in Tuberculosis: Validating Rapid Normalization Kinetics"

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Dynamic Transcriptomic Signatures in Tuberculosis: Validating Rapid Normalization Kinetics" for publication in the IJTLD.

Given the IJTLD's focus on practical solutions for TB control, we believe our findings are highly relevant. We validated a host transcriptomic signature that evaluates treatment response within 7 days—significantly faster than the current gold standard of culture conversion (8 weeks). 

Our multi-cohort analysis (UK and South Africa) confirms that "normalization" of the host immune response (downregulation of inflammation and recovery of T-cell homeostasis) is a robust definition of cure. These findings support the development of non-sputum-based, rapid biomarker tests, which are urgently needed for children and patients with extra-pulmonary TB.

We confirm that this work has not been published elsewhere.
