Subject: Submission of original article "Dynamic Transcriptomic Signatures in Tuberculosis: Validating Rapid Normalization Kinetics"

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Dynamic Transcriptomic Signatures in Tuberculosis: Validating Rapid Normalization Kinetics" for consideration as an Original Article in the Indian Journal of Medical Research.

Tuberculosis remains a critical public health challenge in India and globally. A major barrier to effective control is the lack of rapid, non-sputum-based biomarkers for monitoring treatment efficacy. In this study, we validated a robust host whole-blood transcriptomic signature that detects treatment response as early as Day 7 of therapy, significantly preceding traditional culture conversion.

Using a multi-cohort validation approach (UK and South Africa datasets), we demonstrate that successful cure is characterized by a "normalization storm"—the rapid downregulation of inflammatory genes and the simultaneous physiological recovery of T-cell homeostatic pathways. These findings have significant implications for the design of rapid clinical trials and the management of extra-pulmonary or paucibacillary TB cases, which are common in the Indian setting.

This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the manuscript and there are no conflicts of interest to declare.
